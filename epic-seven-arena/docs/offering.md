# Lady of the Scales: Offering

The cached CeciliaBot S2 description and Offering reference are guarded in `protectionPassiveRules.js`. Changed source descriptions disable the native mapping pending review.

At battle start, Lady grants the Offering unique effect to her team's fixed foremost slot, including herself if she occupies it. There is no substitute if that position is empty. Offering is permanent for the battle while its bearer lives, undispellable and excluded from buff/debuff counts. It is not automatically retained through revival. Ordinary Immunity, Cannot Buff, Block and the manual Sanctuary buff/debuff immunity rule do not prevent or suppress Offering.

When another living teammate receives a direct attack, Offering redirects 70% of the reduced/capped damage to its bearer. Frostbite and attacker sharing bypasses are checked on the original attack target. Frostbite on the Offering bearer does not disable its ability to receive damage for an unaffected teammate. Transferred damage does not receive a second defense/reduction/limit calculation and does not share recursively. User-confirmed: the strongest applicable sharing source wins without addition. A tank configured with 20% team sharing protects other allies; when the third teammate is hit, foremost Lady receives the stronger 70% share, but when Lady herself is directly hit, the tank receives 20%. Damage transferred to Lady does not transfer again to the tank. Equal-strength source ties and simultaneous AoE/death ordering still need verification.

On lethal damage, Offering can activate once. **Per the user's explicit rule**, successful activation preserves up to 1% of the bearer's effective max HP and grants ordinary Immortality for one turn. The source says it grants Immortality on lethal damage but does not specify a numeric HP floor; the 1% value is user-provided, not independently verified. No healing occurs if the bearer was already below that floor. The injury-adjusted maximum after the event is used for the survival floor. Successful survival is not a death or revival and does not trigger death healing, Extinction or death counters.

The protection activates only if Immortality can actually be granted. Cannot Buff, the manual Sanctuary rule and Hecate's active S2 restriction block it. Self-origin is used for the Offering effect's buff grant, so Block's bearer exception applies. A failed grant does not prevent lethal damage. After successful activation, the Offering instance records its use; dispelling Immortality does not reset the one-time trigger. Subsequent hits while ordinary Immortality remains active cannot reduce HP below 1. Existing Immortality is not stripped just by enabling a rule that prevents new grants.

When the Offering bearer dies, its source Lady's living, unsealed passive heals living allies by 50% effective max HP. Shared healing rules apply, including Unhealable, Stigma and injury caps. Death healing resolves before the existing native revival handler. Once granted, Offering's own unique sharing/survival rules are not suppressed merely because Lady or its bearer is sealed.

Hecate's source-guarded S2 restricts all heroes from revival and new Immortality grants while she is living and unsealed. Seal or her defeat disables that aura. Her attack penetration and other skills are not implied by this implementation.

Lady's enemy CR-copy mechanic, S1 healing, and S3 cleanse/CR/HP consumption/Abundance remain pending. Periodic and other secondary damage sharing have not been added outside the established direct-attack pipeline. Undo restores HP, Offering's use flag, Immortality, deaths and restrictions as part of the battle snapshot.
